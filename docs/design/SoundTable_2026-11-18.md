# LAST STANDING: sound replacement table (18 Nov 2026)

Prepared from the code as it stands. Nothing in the repo or in Studio was changed. **Nobody has listened to any of this: every pick below is "to be auditioned in Studio"**, ranked only by title, description, tags and length.

## 1. What the inventory shows

- The game has **86 sound keys** in one table, `src/ReplicatedStorage/Shared/Sounds.lua`, using **91 asset ids**. Replacing a sound is an id change in that file. Nine call sites reuse a key for a second job and need a new key (marked "new key" below).
- **Likely the biggest reason it sounds cheap: Roblox's stock character sounds are probably still playing.** The code mutes only the stock "Died" voice (`CharacterVisuals.lua:555-561`). Nothing in `src` turns off the stock Running, Jumping, Landing, Splash, Swimming, Climbing, FreeFalling and GettingUp sounds, so the plastic stock footstep would play on top of the game's own. Not verified: I was not allowed to run a playtest. Check `Character.HumanoidRootPart` for those Sounds during play.
- **One recording does several jobs at different pitches**, which reads as arcade:
  - "Old clock bell" (118897948050872) is the kill confirm (pitch 1.72), every toast (pitch 2.1) and the feast bell (pitch 0.79).
  - A wood-chopping hit (9120807255) is also the countdown tick (pitch 1.45) and the second arrow impact.
  - A sabre clang is both "equip blade" and the hit marker (pitch 1.38).
  - "Hurt" is the body-hit sound pitched down; "Drop" is the soft landing; "PickUp" is "Equip_Generic" pitched up.
- **Several sources do not match what they stand for:** water steps are "Dinosaur Footsteps In Water"; body hits are "Boxing Hits, glove hits glove"; pick-up is "Plastic Grab, dog head muzzle"; armour is "Body Bag Grab"; the border warning is a "Stadium Buzzer"; the night forest is "Night Jungle"; every blade swing is a "dowel-like" swoosh.
- **A blade or arrow hitting grass, earth, mud, sand or snow plays the wood-chop sound** (`ProjectileController.lua:68-73` maps them all to `Hit_Wood`). There is no earth impact.
- **13 ids are community uploads**, not licensed library audio. They can be removed or made private by their uploader at any time: Draw Bow, bow_release, Audio_arrow_impact, Chest opening, Old clock bell, Breathing and Tired, eat_friedchicken3, Lake Freighter Horn, Ambient wind, Torch_Burn, and three "ui_tap" sounds (Google Material Design, CC-BY 4.0, which needs a credit line).
- **The footsteps are from one set by "AmbientSorcery"** (10 "CharacterAudio" loops, an ID-verified creator, not a Roblox licensed partner as far as the store shows). The licensed Pro Sound Effects library on Roblox has no clean human footsteps on grass, earth, rock or wood (only dragon, donkey, robot and dinosaur). So the footsteps stay on that set unless they are recorded or bought and uploaded; only the water steps have a licensed replacement.

### How candidates were found
- Studio's `search_asset` (Creator Store, Audio) was tried first. It returns mostly community uploads, many ripped from other games (Half-Life 2, CS:GO, SCP), so none of those are used.
- All candidates below come from the same public Creator Store search, restricted to Roblox's licensed partners: **PSE = ProSoundEffects** (creator id 7462895450) and **APM = APMOfficial** (7462718749). Name, id, length and creator were read back from the store's asset details for every id. All are free.
- Lengths are the store's figures. Many PSE files hold several takes in a row ("multiple", "various versions", "twice"): those need `regions` slices in `Sounds.lua`, measured in Studio.

### Legend
- **Vol** = `volume` in `Sounds.lua` (0..10) × the multiplier at the call site. **Roll** = `rollMin`–`rollMax` in studs, InverseTapered. Positional sounds without their own figures use 10–160 (`Sfx.lua:77-78`).
- **2D** = no position (heard at full level by that player only). **3D** = positional.
- Files: `SND` = `src/ReplicatedStorage/Shared/Sounds.lua`; `AC` = `…/Client/Controllers/AudioController.lua`; `CC` = `CombatController.lua`; `PC` = `ProjectileController.lua`; `IC` = `InventoryController.lua`; `NC` = `NotificationController.lua`; `LC` = `LaunchController.lua`; `LIC` = `LaunchIntroController/init.lua`; `FRC` = `FallenRecapController/init.lua`; `ArC` = `ArenaController/init.lua` (all under `src/StarterPlayer/StarterPlayerScripts/Client/Controllers/`); `CS`, `IS`, `LS`, `PS`, `RS`, `AS`, `WP` = `CombatService.lua`, `InventoryService.lua`, `LootService.lua`, `PlayerService.lua`, `RoundService.lua`, `ArenaService/init.lua`, `ProjectileService/WorldProjectiles.lua` (under `src/ServerScriptService/Server/Services/`).
- **Verdict**: REPLACE, KEEP (already a good licensed fit, audition anyway), or SPLIT (give the second job its own key).

## 11. What the age rating needs

The game is to be published for all ages with no blood and no wounds (OwnerRequests.md, rows 26 and 60). For sound that means:

- **No pain cries, screams, grunts or death voices.** Today this holds: `Hurt` is a body thud, `Death` is a body fall and is not even played, and Roblox's stock "Died" voice is muted (`CharacterVisuals.lua:557`). Keep it that way when the body-fall sound is wired in.
- **No wet or tearing impact sounds.** These turned up in the searches and must not be used: `Flesh Stab` (wet, with knife), `Pig Gross Effects` (twisting knife in wound), `Squishy Bone Cracks`, `Bone Cracks`, `Sever Metal Hit` (cut off hand, arm), `Body Hit Slap` (wet, gooey), `Body Fall Master` takes described as "occasional wet impact" (the two in use now, 43 and 53, need a listen for that).
- **Breathing is the only voice in the game.** Keep it as effort (out of breath), never distress. Avoid `Wheezing Strained Breathing`, `Bag Breath` (panic attack), `Gasp … Drowning`.
- **Hits on a player stay dry**: cloth and padded thuds only, on the attacker's hit marker as well.
- **No gun-like sounds** for arrows: avoid `Bullet Whiz` and `Rico`.
- **Crowd beds** for the lobby must not carry intelligible speech; audition `Crowd Murmur` for that.
- **The owner's own TRIBUTE document asks for a scream on severing.** That whole part (blood, severing) is switched off by the all-ages decision, so no sound is prepared for it.
- **Community uploads ripped from other games** (Half-Life 2, CS:GO, SCP sets appear in every Studio search) are an ownership risk as well as a quality one. None are proposed.

## 12. Order of work when the replacement starts

1. Check and silence the stock character sounds; listen again before changing anything else.
2. Replace the 13 community uploads (bow, arrow impact, crate, bell, horn, wind, fire, eat, breathing, UI taps).
3. Give the reused sounds their own assets (hit marker, kill confirm, countdown, notify, hurt, drop, pick-up, dry fire, crate landing).
4. Fix the mismatches (water steps, body hits, swings, armour, border warning, night bed, lake bed).
5. Add the gaps: earth impact, cloth and gear, body fall, launch rise and sun-glare arrival, board UI.
6. Audition every pick in Studio, cut `regions` for multi-take files, then re-balance volumes with the AudioAnalyzer as the current table was.
