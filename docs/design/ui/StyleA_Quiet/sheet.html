Style A: Quiet Almost nothing on screen. The valley is the screen.

1. In a round

Nothing sits in a box, so the valley shows through everywhere. Health, hunger and thirst are three thin lines, bottom left. A line turns amber only when it runs low, and then an amber drop on the compass points to water. Small picture, bottom right: what you see when you step outside the border.

2. The lobby

The top half of the screen stays pure picture. Title and countdown bottom left, who is here bottom right, three plain text buttons. ROBLOX PLUS is the quietest of the three.

3. When you are defeated

No card pops up. One thin strip along the bottom says who defeated you and where you placed, and lets you watch the players still in. The full results wait for you on the board in the lobby.

4. The results board in the lobby

A real board you walk up to. Same thin lettering, as dark ink on a matte off-white panel so it reads in daylight. Your own row is marked in amber. Left: the board's face. Right: the board on its wooden stand.
