# Store text: the description and the title (draft, 18 November 2026)

*For you to choose from. Nothing here has been put on Roblox. It goes in at Creator Dashboard → the game → Publish settings → Game details ([PublishChecklist.md](PublishChecklist.md), section 4.1, step 3).*

## The description

Roblox's limit is 1,000 characters; this is 795. It names no film and no character, says nothing about injuries, and doesn't depend on which title you pick.

```text
A first-person survival arena, in beta.

Up to 48 contenders rise onto plinths around the Horn in a green mountain valley. When the signal sounds, rush the Horn for gear or run for the hills.

- Open crates for weapons and supplies: sword, spear, bow, throwing knives and more.
- Watch your hunger and thirst. Drink from the stream or the lake.
- Day turns to night, and the border closes in on whoever is left.
- If you fall, you can watch the rest of the round. The last one standing wins.

On your own? You get a practice round with training dummies while you wait for other players.

THIS IS A BETA IN TESTING. Things will change, and some will break. It is built for PC, phone and controller; phone and controller are still being tested.
```

*The "no pay-to-win" line was taken out on 18 November: you decided that epic items from the wheel and loot boxes will be stronger.*

**What each line rests on**
- "Up to 48", the plinths, the Horn, the signal, crates, hunger and thirst, the stream and the lake, night, the border, spectating and the practice round are all in the game today ([Status.md](Status.md)).
- "phone and controller are still being tested" is true as written: neither has been tried on a real device.
- "nothing you can buy changes a fight": the only things connected to money are the Roblox Plus button, whose perk is a mark in the lobby, and private servers.
- Leave out the phone and controller sentence, or change it, once those tests are done.

## Three titles that don't use another owner's brand

The title you chose on 14 November is "HUNGER GAMES (BETA)", knowing it's a registered trademark; today the experience is named "HUNGER GAMES (UNDER DEVELOPMENT + TESTING)". Roblox removes content that infringes or is confusingly similar, so here are three alternatives made from the game's own words. Whether to change is your call.

1. **LAST STANDING (BETA)**: the working title, already on the lobby screen with the tagline "Outlast everyone".
2. **48 CONTENDERS (BETA)**: says the size of a round at a glance.
3. **THE HORN: Survival Arena (BETA)**: named after the map's centre, with the genre for search.

**Not checked:** none of the three was searched on Roblox or against trademark registers, so another game may already use one of them.

The icon is a separate point: it is a golden bird in a ring, close to the films' pin ([Status.md](Status.md), "Still open").
